# WyrdStoryletTie
Generated by tools/gen_api_docs.sh from the ## doc comments. Do not edit by hand.

**Experimental**

Inherits: `RefCounted`

Compile-time warning when two storylets can tie.

A pair warns when the scope matches, the speakers can meet in one query, priority and specificity are equal, and the conditions can both be true. Weight does not suppress the warning. `speaker: any` overlaps every speaker in that scope. Two other speakers do not overlap. Exclusion is equality on one subject, `!=`, and `not` of the same comparison or call. `not` of `and` or `or` does not prove exclusion. An `or` does not either.
