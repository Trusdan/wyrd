# WyrdEffectDef
Generated by tools/gen_api_docs.sh from the ## doc comments. Do not edit by hand.

**Experimental**

Inherits: `WyrdStmtSpan`

One slice effect. Plain data, with type-checked expressions.

[`kind`](#kind) is `set`, `inc`, `record_deed`, `unlock_topic`, `lock_topic`, `do`, `outcome`, `believe`, `unbelieve`, `declare`, `retract`, `learn`, `forget`, `start_quest`, `advance`, `assign_role`, `vacate_role`, `adjust_baseline`, or `create_document`. [`quest`](#quest) and [`stage`](#stage) are the quest-flow target. [`role`](#role) is the role id. [`entity`](#entity) is the assignee. [`axis`](#axis), [`a`](#a), and [`b`](#b) are the baseline edge. The amount of `adjust_baseline` is [`value`](#value). [`agent`](#agent) is the belief or knowledge agent, or the group id. A deed with `reveals` stores the fact on [`fact_key`](#fact_key) and the claim on [`value`](#value). `statement: true` stays in [`args`](#args). [`args`](#args) holds named deed or command arguments. Command names are checked against `WyrdCommandSchema` when the linker has one.
